HOW YOU OBSERVE A PIECE

After you write, stay on one paragraph. Answer three questions.

1. What parts does this result depend on, and where did you stop at the tool?
2. Which lines are your narration, and which lines are a catalog, a pasted install block, or a paraphrase of a complex sentence?
3. Is this a habit, or is it only this post?

491 is the shape of a good answer to the first question. You name the encoding from its marks, then you stop at CyberChef. That stop is the observation. "This feels early" is not.

978 is the shape of a good answer to the second. The CTF lab is yours. The second-order section in the same file is not, and it has no White box heading. You mark the boundary. You do not re-read the whole year.

The third question keeps a one-off out of the permanent rule. One sentence you dislike does not become a ban.
