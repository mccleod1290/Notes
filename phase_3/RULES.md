# Phase 3 rules

**Phase 3 is the exam.**

Phase 2 made the page. This phase finds out whether he can use it. Two tracks, both required. Neither track is allowed inside `phase_2/`.

## Practical

He builds one work item from `phase_1/output/<slug>/MAP.md`, aimed at what `phase_1/output/<slug>/TARGET.md` says done looks like. The brief names the thing, the constraint, and what "done" looks like. He uploads the result. The judge says what holds and what does not. Judgment is specific. No score theater.

After that, or instead when he asks, a spar. One Socratic question at a time, taken from the page, aimed at a hole. Stop when the hole has a name. Do not hand him the answer. Do not lecture.

## Academic

Three papers, together. Not one mixed sheet.

| Paper | What he writes |
| --- | --- |
| MCQ | 15 questions. Four options. One answer. |
| Short | 4 questions. 180 words each. Band is 150 to 200. |
| Long | 2 essays. 500 words each. Band is 450 to 600. Graduation level: he argues the mechanism, he does not list it. |

The printed paper has no answers. The key stays in `KEY.md` and is never emailed.

Questions are domains on `phase_1/output/<slug>/MAP.md`. The phase 2 page has to be able to support the question. A domain the map dropped is not on the paper and not in the build. If the map or the page is missing, stop.

## Weekend

When he asks for the paper, write the three papers and the build brief, render them, and email the papers to `dailyupdatesforstudies@gmail.com` unless he names another address. He prints, writes, and uploads into `phase_3/output/<slug>/uploads/`.

The mail is the three papers and the build brief. It is not the key. It is not a spar.

## Not this phase

Do not rewrite the phase 2 page here. Do not add a quiz to that page.

Whether he should sit this phase again is a phase 4 call. The diagnostician writes that route. The examiner does not.
